# Phase 8: The story

This is the Phase 8 design, as the player approved it (2026-09-25). ARCHITECTURE.md §12
describes what was built.

## The player's answers

1. **The opening:** a short illustrated intro, straight into Haven.
2. **The villain:** the admiral whose seal is on Blackwood's letter ordered the attack.
   The revenge arc ends in a set-piece battle against his flagship. His name has to be
   discovered in play.
3. **The black flag:** a choice made mid-game.
4. **Telling it:** a journal of quests. Characters add their own words to its entries,
   as extra detail.
5. **The pictures:** AI-painted panels from the player's ComfyUI (Nano Banana Pro).

## The story

**Intro:** five painted panels, with captions.
1. A foundling on Haven's quay is taken in by Captain Elias Thorne of the merchant
   brig *Good Hope*.
2. You grow up aboard her.
3. An Imperial squadron under a black-and-gold admiral's pennant runs her down at dawn.
4. She burns. Thorne's last words: "Blackwood knew… find what he buried."
5. You wake in Haven. Nell Brandt, the harbourmaster, gives you Thorne's old sloop.

**The journal's thread:**
1. **Ashes of the *Good Hope*.** Nell, at Haven's Guildhall, sends you to Jonas Quill,
   the *Good Hope*'s bosun, in the pirate haven.
2. **The bosun's tale.** Quill describes the squadron and its flagship, the
   *Sovereign*. Thorne told him the proof lay in Blackwood's hoard. Quill also names
   Tobias Finch, a code clerk the Crown turned out.
3. **Blackwood's chart.** The Phase 7 treasure hunt. Its three pieces lead to the
   hoard, and to the sealed letter in the Crown's cipher.
4. **The Crown's cipher.** Finch, in the nearer Imperial port's tavern after dark,
   reads the letter. **Lord Admiral Harrow** sold the Crown's silver to Blackwood, then
   sent the *Sovereign* to sink the *Good Hope* when Thorne found out.
5. **The black flag.** You choose how to go after him:
   - **Raise the black flag** with Red Mary Kincaid of the Brethren. Pirates take you
     in, the Crown outlaws you, and the Guild cools. Your crew is made up, your gunners
     drilled, and two of her ships (*Revenge*, *Gull*) sail with you against the
     *Sovereign*.
   - **Keep your colours** with the Guild's letter of marque from Nell. The Crown's
     regard holds, the Guild's warms, your crew is made up, and the Guild's
     shipwrights stiffen your hull.
6. **The *Sovereign*.** She lies off the Crown's capital with two brigs, marked on the
   chart. Beat her, board her, and duel Harrow on his own deck, the hardest duel in the
   game. Sink her instead and he goes down with her.

**The end:** an epilogue picture, with words for each road and for sinking her.
